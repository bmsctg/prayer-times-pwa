    Device Setup & Help Guide

    Follow these simple steps for your device to get background prayer alerts, 100% reliable alarms, and live Qibla compass.

    1
    Add to Home Screen (Safari)

    Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari, scroll down, and select "Add to Home Screen".

    2
    Enable Push Alerts (iOS 16.4+)

    Open the app from your Home Screen, toggle on "Web & Push Alerts", and tap Allow when prompted for notifications.

    3
    Guaranteed Alarms (Apple Calendar)

    Tap "Sync / Update Selected Alarms in Calendar". Safari will prompt to subscribe/import 30 days of prayer alarms straight into your iPhone's Apple Calendar with lock-screen alerts.

    4
    Live Qibla Compass

    Tap "Enable Live Device Compass" and choose Allow when iOS requests Motion & Orientation sensor access.

    1
    Install App (Chrome / Samsung)

    Tap browser menu (⋮ or ≡) and select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

    2
    Push & Background Alerts

    Turn on "Web & Push Alerts" and tap Allow. For uninterrupted notifications, ensure battery optimization for this app is set to 'Unrestricted' in Android Settings.

    3
    Guaranteed Lock-Screen Alarms

    Tap "Sync / Update Selected Alarms in Calendar" to download the `.ics` file, then open it with Google Calendar or your calendar app to import all 30 days of prayer alarms.

    4
    Live Qibla Compass

    Ensure Location (GPS) is enabled on your phone. Tap "Enable Live Device Compass" and grant sensor permissions.

    1
    Install Desktop PWA

    Click the Install icon in your browser address bar (Chrome / Edge / Brave) to install Prayer Times as a desktop app.

    2
    Desktop Notifications

    Enable "Web & Push Alerts" to get system tray notifications for prayer times even when the browser tab is closed.

    3
    Download Monthly Schedule

    Use "Download PDF Document" to save or print a full A4 30-day prayer timetable.
